ARCADE RULES

How to play

🔢 Merge Streak (4×4):
Swipe or use arrow keys to combine matching numbers (2+2=4, 4+4=8...). Keep building toward 2048+!

🧱 Block Collapse (8×8):
Tap any shape in your rack, then tap the grid to place it. Complete horizontal rows or vertical columns to clear them. Clear multiple lines together for explosive combo bonuses!

🔮 Color Cascade (7×7):
Tap a gem, then tap an adjacent neighbor to swap them. Line up 3 or more matching gems to trigger cascades and build combo multipliers across 30 moves!

🧠 Memory Matrix (3×3):
Watch the light sequence flash across the 9 neon pads. When it's your turn, tap the pads in the identical sequence. Clear rounds to earn points and advance. You have 3 lives!

🐍 Neon Viper (16×16):
Steer the neon viper with arrows, D-pad, or swipe gestures to collect energy pellets. Each pellet increases length and score. Avoid crashing into walls or your own tail!
