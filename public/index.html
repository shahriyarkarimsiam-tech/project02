Merge your way up.

Swipe or use arrow controls to move the tiles. Matching numbers merge into one larger number and add their value to your score.

Classic mode: Save your run any time after two moves. Daily: One seeded attempt per UTC day on Telegram. Finishing the daily run uses up today's entry.

Coins: Earned after verified game saves and only redeemable for cosmetic themes. No money value or withdrawals.
